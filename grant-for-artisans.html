About the Program

Artisan Empowerment Grant

The ALIF Grant for Artisans was established to recognize and uplift the immense contribution of skilled tradesmen and women to Nigeria's economy. From tailors and carpenters to welders, mechanics, and food processors — our artisans are the backbone of community life.

Through targeted grant packages, ALIF provides eligible artisans with starter kits, equipment, and business training to scale their craft. We believe that when skilled individuals are properly equipped and supported, entire communities thrive.

Our program also offers mentorship, market linkage support, and follow-up visits to ensure grants translate into lasting, sustainable improvements in beneficiaries' lives and livelihoods.

Tools & Equipment Grants

Direct provision of professional tools and equipment to qualifying artisans to enhance productivity and quality of work.

Business & Skills Training

Structured training in financial literacy, pricing, record-keeping, and business development tailored to artisan needs.

Mentorship & Market Linkage

Connecting artisans with experienced mentors and market opportunities to expand their customer base and income.
