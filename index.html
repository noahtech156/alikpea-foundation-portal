Welcome to Agbonjagwe Leemon Ikpea Foundation

ALIF is dedicated to empowering Nigeria's most vulnerable—providing scholarships for indigent students, supporting widows and the elderly, and offering free medical care to those in need. Since 2012, our mission has been to uplift lives through education, compassion, and community service. Together with our partners and volunteers, we strive to create lasting impact and brighter futures for all.

ALIF AT A GLANCE

WHo WE ARE

Agbonjawe Leemon Ikepa Foundation (ALIF) was founded by Chief (Dr.) Leemon A. Ikpea FNSE, CON,JP, Group Chairman/CEO,Lee Enginering Group and Allied Companies Limited. ALIF was officially inaugurated on November 17, 2012 with sincere desire to benefit humanity through charitable activities which includes:

Scholarship

Scholarship

Extending scholarship scheme to indigent students and young persons of school age, especially orphans. Our scholarship scheme, which is the flagship of our program, runs through the six years of secondary school up to first degree level and is open to eligible indigent students in any public secondary and tertiary institution, polytechnic & college of education in Nigeria.

Beneficiaries of the scholarship program, apart from the financial assistance, also enjoy the following benefits:

  • Periodic academic development and social events throughout the year
  • Supplementary tutoring and academic remediation where necessary
  • Access to a network of ALIF scholarship and foundation members
  • Career guidance and mentoring

Youth and Community Dev.

Youth Development

ALIF promotes youth employment through skills acquisition, small business support packs for artisans, as well as grassroots football development programs for rural youth who desire to take football as a career.

Grassroot Football means bringing together children and giving them access to football in their own environment, whatever age, gender, physical condition, religion, or ethnic group. At ALIF, we have adopted this means to curb youth restiveness. We had our test run during the first anniversary of ALIF, where we engaged youths in our communities in a football competition amongst the 5 Local Government Council Areas in Esan Land in Edo state.

ALIF Free Medical Services

Rural Health Care Services

ALIF facilitates easy access to primary health care services for people living in rural communities. We run regular medical outreaches and free screenings focused on prevention and early treatment of common illnesses.

Besides sponsoring several people for medical treatment both in Nigeria and abroad, ALIF addresses issues pertaining to good health and vitality through our medical outreaches where prevention of diet-related diseases as well as their management are taught by professionals drawn from relevant fields of medicine.

We conduct free medical screening for indigent members of our communities. They are majorly screened for:

  • Skin diseases
  • Respiratory tract infections
  • Eye diseases
  • Malaria
  • HIV
  • Hepatitis
  • Diabetes

Free drugs are usually distributed to participants and serious cases are followed up to ensure the individual gets proper medical attention at no cost to them. ALIF is currently building a 60-bed capacity health facility in Ewatto, Esan South East LGA of Nigeria.

Grant for Artisans

Grant for Artisans

ALIF supports artisans and small business owners with grant packages that include tools, materials, and training to strengthen rural enterprise and increase household income.

Our grant program provides eligible artisans with starter kits, equipment, and targeted training to scale their craft. We also offer mentorship, business guidance, and market linkage support so beneficiaries can grow sustainable livelihoods.

The program is designed to empower creative tradesmen, encourage self-reliance, and build economic resilience in underserved communities. Follow-up visits and impact monitoring help ensure grants translate into lasting improvements.

Mentoring Program

Mentoring Program

ALIF provides mentoring programs that connect young people with experienced professionals, helping them build confidence, career direction, and practical skills.

Through workshops, coaching sessions, and community gatherings, our mentors teach goal-setting, leadership, and resilience. Participants receive personalised guidance to help them navigate education, entrepreneurship, and employment pathways.

The mentoring program empowers youth and vulnerable adults to make better decisions, seize opportunities, and contribute positively to their communities.

OUR VALUE

The core values of ALIF are captured by the acronym C.R.E.D.I.T. These values form the foundation of our work, guiding every program, outreach, and support service we deliver to our nation’s most vulnerable people.

C

Courage

Standing strong for vulnerable communities and taking bold action in service of others.

R

Responsibility

Taking ownership of our commitments and delivering meaningful impact with integrity.

E

Empathy

Listening deeply to the needs of the less privileged and responding with care.

D

Determination

Persisting through challenges to create access to education, health, and opportunity.

I

Integrity

Operating transparently and honourably in every program we deliver.

T

Tolerance

Respecting every life and promoting inclusion across our communities.

OUR MISSION

To provide access to functional education, skills development and health care for the less privileged individuals in our society.

OUR VISION

To become the most dependable and available platform for less privileged persons to attain a life of meaning and worth.

TEAM MEMBERS

The management of ALIF is spearheaded by a team of dedicated and visionary leaders. At the helm of the foundation's establishment is its founder, Chief (Dr.) Leemon A. Ikepa, a successful business tycoon, renowned philanthropist and humanitarian.

The Board of Trustees, comprising distinguished individuals, provides strategic guidance and oversight. Notably, Rt. Hon. Matthew Egbadon, former speaker Edo state House of Assembly, Principal Partner at Highfliers Solicitors and a respected member of the board, brings his wealth of expertise to the table.

The day-to-day operations of ALIF are piloted by its Executive Director, Dr Difference Irabor, a former Banking Executive at Fidelity Bank, who holds a Master of Business Administration; is a Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Human Resource Management (CIHRM); a Chartered Human Resource Business Partner (Ch.HRBP); and is currently undergoing a Certified Career Analyst (CCA) course at Edumilstone, Bangalore, India. Since the Foundation's inception, Dr. Irabor has demonstrated exceptional leadership, driving ALIF's mission forward with passion and dedication.

Together, this leadership team has positioned ALIF for impactful service, driving positive change and making a lasting difference in the lives of those they serve.
