About the Programme

The ALIF Scholarship

Our scholarship scheme — the flagship of the ALIF programme — runs through secondary school up to first degree level. It is open to eligible indigent students in public secondary schools, polytechnics, colleges of education, and universities across Nigeria.

Our Mission

To provide access to functional education for less privileged individuals, removing financial barriers that prevent talented students from realising their full potential.

What Scholars Receive

  • ✓ Financial assistance for tuition and academic needs
  • ✓ Periodic academic development events
  • ✓ Supplementary tutoring and academic remediation
  • ✓ Access to the ALIF alumni and scholarship network
  • ✓ Career guidance and professional mentoring
Who Can Apply

Eligibility Criteria

Enrolled Student

Must be currently enrolled in a recognised Nigerian secondary or tertiary institution

Valid Admission

Must have a valid admission letter from an accredited Nigerian institution

Academic Excellence

Demonstrated academic merit and commitment to educational achievement

Financial Need

Demonstrated financial need — scholarship targets indigent students and orphans

Nigerian Citizen

Must be a Nigerian citizen studying in a Nigerian educational institution

Complete Documents

Must provide all required documents including WAEC, JAMB, and admission letter
